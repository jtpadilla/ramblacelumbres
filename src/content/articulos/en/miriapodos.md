Myriapods are the earliest of the jawed arthropods. They have not developed the waxy layer of arachnids and insects, so they lose water by evaporation: they have to live by night and spend the day under stones. They have numerous pairs of jointed legs, like those of arachnids, for walking along the ground.

## Scutigera coleoptrata

![Scutigera coleoptrata](../../../assets/uploads/2014/07/Scutigera-coleoptrata.jpg "*Scutigera coleoptrata*.")

Outdoors it lives in cool, damp places: in cracks in the rocks, under leaf litter and in tree bark. It is also found in houses, where it looks for damp places such as cellars and bathrooms.
