Mantodeans or mantises are insects we recognise at first sight by their characteristic appearance. They have evolved to confuse their predators by adapting to the shapes of their surroundings. They have a very mobile head, grasping forelegs suited to catching prey, and short, generally very fine antennae. They live in dry scrubland. The order comprises some 2,000 species.

![Ameles spallanzania](../../../assets/uploads/2014/06/Ameles-spallanzania-1.jpg "*Ameles spallanzania*.")
![Empusa pennata, female](../../../assets/uploads/2014/06/Empusa-penata-2-hembra.jpg "*Empusa pennata*, female.")

The *Empusa pennata* or conehead mantis is larger. It bears the name of a spectre of Greek mythology who, with the appearance of a beautiful young woman, concealed her bloodthirsty instincts.

![Empusa pennata, male](../../../assets/uploads/2014/06/Empusa-penata-3-macho.jpg "*Empusa pennata*, male.")
