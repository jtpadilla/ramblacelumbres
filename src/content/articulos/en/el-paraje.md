![Landscape of the Rambla Celumbres](../../../assets/uploads/2014/03/1.jpg)

The Rambla Celumbres Municipal Nature Reserve lies in the Els Ports region of Castellón. It takes in parts of the municipalities of Castellfort, Portell de Morella and Cinctorres, and with 1,194.40 hectares it is one of the largest municipal nature reserves in the Valencian Community.

Some 10,000 different plant species are thought to grow here, of which 6,500 are native and 1,500 endemic. Wildlife is abundant too: invertebrates, vertebrates and microorganisms.

To appreciate how sheer the Roca Parda and the Roca Roja are, there is nothing better than approaching them along the Rambla Celumbres. This rambla rarely carries water, but when it rains the landscape is quite unlike the one we are used to seeing: the greys and ochres of the rocks shine, the green of the trees is luminous; nothing like when everything is dry and scorched by drought and sun.

![The Roca Roja with the rambla carrying water](../../../assets/uploads/2014/03/10.jpg "The Roca Roja. The Rambla Celumbres with water.")

The oldest materials in the area, from the Jurassic and the Tertiary, are found in the Rambla Celumbres. Its formation is rocky, of calcareous dolomites: fluids rich in magnesium seeped into them and transformed them chemically, losing calcium carbonate and gaining magnesium. The erosion of its visible materials and their sedimentation are the gravel, sand and clay that its waters carry along.

The right-hand side, the Roca Roja, belongs to the municipality of Portell, and the Roca Parda, on the left, to Cinctorres.
