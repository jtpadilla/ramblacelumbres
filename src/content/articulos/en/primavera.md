It is one of the four seasons of the year and, in Spanish, the only one with a feminine name: *primavera* means “first green”.

This year winter ended on 20 March at 23:45, with a solar eclipse that we could not see here because of the cloud all day long. From the spring equinox until 21 June 2015 the day grows almost three minutes longer each day in the peninsula, and the plants that have been overwintering wake up: they begin a period of growth and flowering, their activity starts.

![Field in spring](../../../assets/uploads/2015/03/5-1.jpg)
![Spring flowers](../../../assets/uploads/2015/03/3-2.jpg)

The green of the whole made up of trees, shrubs and every kind of vegetation is luminous, clean.

Bulbous plants send up the stems and leaves they lost in winter. Their bulbs are organs below ground where they store the nutrient reserves that feed the new plant.

Deciduous trees put out their leaves again, giving shade and holding in moisture. Walking through the countryside you can see that the land is exuberant; every few days the colours change. I do not know why, but blues and yellows dominate among the flowers; the fields of almond trees in blossom are a broad carpet of white or pale pink, depending on whether the fruit is *marcona* or *mollar*, and on the banks and in the ploughed land the deep red of the poppies looks like drops of blood bringing life among the green of the vegetation. It is a spectacle that changes from day to day.
